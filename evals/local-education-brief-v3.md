REPEATING PATTERNS

- **Clean CEO/CTO two-person split, technical co-founder owns the model.** Recurs across eras: Clever (Carroll CPO / Garcia CTO), Scout (Fichter CEO / Bertfield CTO), Edexia (Gibbon CEO / Wang CTO), Frizzle (Gupta CEO ex-PM / Sai CTO ex-MLE), Educato (Lemeni CEO / Gabler CTO ML).
- **Domain-credentialed math/science founders attacking tutoring.** Deep subject expertise, not just ML skill, is the stated edge: Chiron (two Oxford mathematicians, neurosymbolics), Excellence Learning (International Physicists' Tournament winner plus Oxford maths MSc who tutored 10+ years), Mathos (ML researcher, college at 14).
- **Teenage / very-young repeat edtech founders.** Edexia's Gibbon and Wang each built an edtech to "200k ARR at 18 [and 17]"; General Learning's Michael Tong is 19 and already a co-founder/CTO; Units' Martinez was Forbes-covered at 18 — a distinctly YC-education profile.
- **Consultant/big-tech operator pairs with an institutional-domain insider.** Risely AI pairs a Bain/Airwallex/NerdWallet GTM operator with a Salesforce Education Cloud PM and former UC Regent; Alice.tech's Rants is ex-McKinsey AI plus 11 years of university teaching; Bloomy's Southmayd is McKinsey plus Teach For America.
- **Institution-as-buyer infrastructure thesis, restated a decade later.** Clever (S12) and Panorama (S13) sold into districts; Scout (W25) explicitly frames "digital infrastructure for America's schools," and Risely AI targets universities — the same buyer, new stack.
- **Repeat-founder and prior-education-nonprofit distribution.** CodeCombat's Winter previously built Skritter; Miyagi Labs' Davis previously founded The Gift of Chess for global chess education; Frizzle's Sai ran the Midwest Math Circle for 10 years.

EMERGING / NEW PATTERNS

- **Tech approach shifts from content/marketplace to model-native tutoring and generation.** Older set = curriculum, marketplaces, games (Codecademy, Outschool, Cambly, CodeCombat); 2024–25 set = AI tutors and automated content (Tegore "personalized AI Tutor for K-12," Miyagi "turn videos into complete and interactive courses," Educato AI exam prep).
- **Explicit non-LLM technical differentiation appears.** Chiron's "neurosymbolics for math" signals recent founders trying to escape commodity-LLM tutoring — no analogue in the older cohort.
- **Founder profile bifurcates.** Recent cohorts include both very young repeat edtech founders (Edexia, General Learning, Units) and senior enterprise operators (Risely AI, Alice.tech, Bloomy, Frizzle) — a wider spread than the pre-2017 group, whose bios are largely absent here.
- **Internationalization.** Copenhagen, London, Brisbane and Hong Kong teams all appear in Fall 2024–Spring 2025; no non-US location appears in the pre-2017 cohort in this evidence.
- Caveat: era comparison is constrained because most pre-2017 entries have empty bios and no product descriptions, so "older cohort" claims rest on company identity rather than evidence text.
