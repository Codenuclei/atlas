REPEATING PATTERNS

- **Teacher-time-reclaimed as the enterprise wedge.** The most fundable school-facing pitch is eliminating a specific hour-consuming teacher or admin workflow rather than replacing instruction: Frizzle (S25) grades handwritten math and cites 10+ hours/week lost to grading; Edexia (W25) grades essays with a teacher-trainable rubric model; Scout (W25) automates attendance-to-compliance busywork; Risely (S25) targets $735B of university administrative labor.
- **Founder-was-the-tutor / founder-was-the-teacher.** Personal pedagogical experience is the recurring authorization to build: Chiron's Booth brothers tutored for a decade and are Oxford math grads; Excellence Learning's Sovann Linden tutored 10+ years; Alice.tech's Kim Rants taught university students 11+ years; Bloomy's Alex Southmayd taught 7th grade with Teach For America and at Manhattan Prep; Univerbal's Philipp Hadjimina traces the product to his own 1-on-1 Greek lessons.
- **"Duolingo for X" consumerization of a formerly institutional category.** Multiple companies explicitly borrow consumer-app mechanics for academic content: Tegore describes a "Duolingo for Math," Alice.tech calls itself "Duolingo for exams," and Miyagi and Educato apply the same practice-engine framing to standardized tests — with Educato's twist being the long tail of non-US exams.
- **CEO/CTO two-person split with one domain owner and one systems builder.** The dominant founding-team shape: Risely (Saifuddin owns Education Cloud/SIS domain and buyer access; Abbasi owns enterprise GTM), Frizzle (Gupta PM/commercial, Sai ML), Toko (Liu ex-Quizlet product, Du ex-Palantir/Google engineering), SuperKalam (Rathore ex-Unacademy growth, Gupta ex-Razorpay engineering), Clever (Carroll product, Garcia engineering).
- **Emerging-market and non-US-exam distribution as first market.** Several companies deliberately start outside the US K-12/SAT core: Darsel delivers K-12 math over SMS/WhatsApp in resource-constrained settings; Miyagi supports UTME (Nigeria), KCSE/KJSEA/KPSEA (Kenya) and NEET/CAT (India); Toko launched in Taiwan and ranks top-3 in its App Store Education category; MySivi and SuperKalam are both Bengaluru-based and India-market-first.
- **Ex-consultant / ex-big-tech operator entering edtech.** Consulting and platform-scale operator backgrounds recur: McKinsey appears in Alice.tech (Rants), Bloomy (Southmayd) and Darsel (Haidar); Bain at Risely (Abbasi); big-tech PM/ML at Frizzle (Coinbase/Tesla/Meta; Microsoft/Meta/Google).

EMERGING / NEW PATTERNS

- **From plumbing to judgment.** The older cohort sold connective infrastructure and data collection — Clever (SSO/rostering, S12), Panorama (surveys/analytics, S13), GiveCampus (fundraising rails, S15). The 2025 cohort sells systems that make or execute decisions: Risely's "specialized AI agents" acting inside SIS/LMS/CRM, Scout's AI-native SIS, Edexia's rubric-learning grader that updates from teacher corrections. Same buyer, far deeper into the workflow.
- **Model-level and neurosymbolic differentiation replacing content libraries.** Older companies competed on content and network (Codecademy's course library, Outschool's teacher supply, Cambly's tutor supply).
